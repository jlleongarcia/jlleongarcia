## About me

Medical physicist by profession, tinkerer by habit.

I write to keep my **learning process structured** — and because documenting the setup, the mistakes, and the reasoning is far more useful than just the final command.

Everything here came from a real problem I had to solve. If you're starting your own journey, I hope something saves you the hours it cost me.
